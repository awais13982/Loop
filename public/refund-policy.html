Refund Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

At LOOP, we want you to be satisfied with your subscription. If you're not happy with your purchase, you may request a full refund within 14 days of your initial subscription purchase.

How to request a refund

Since Paddle.com acts as our Merchant of Record and handles all payments, refund requests are processed through Paddle. You can request a refund by contacting Paddle support directly, or by emailing us at support@bonto.run and we'll help coordinate it with Paddle.

What's covered

  • Full refunds are available within 14 days of your first subscription payment for any plan (Pro, Business, or Team).
  • Refunds are not available for renewal payments after the initial 14-day window. You can cancel your subscription at any time to stop future billing — your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method and are typically processed within 5–10 business days by Paddle.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@bonto.run.
