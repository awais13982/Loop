LOOP

Privacy Policy · Last updated September 21, 2026

LOOP ("we", "our", "the app") is an AI-powered work assistant that helps freelancers, agencies, and small businesses keep track of unfinished work — follow-ups, payments, and deadlines — hidden in client conversations. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to run your account and analyze the conversations you choose to bring into LOOP:

  • Account information: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Client and project data you enter: client names, project details, pasted conversation text, and any notes you add.
  • Connected accounts (optional): if you connect Gmail, Slack, WhatsApp, or Messenger, we access message content and metadata from those accounts solely to identify open loops (unfinished work, pending payments, missed deadlines) within them.
  • Billing information: if you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number — Stripe handles that directly.
  • Usage data: basic technical logs (e.g. login times, feature usage) used to keep the service running reliably.

2. How we use your information

  • To analyze conversations (pasted or synced) and surface what's stuck, why, and what to do next.
  • To send you reminders, follow-up suggestions, and a weekly work-health report.
  • To process subscription payments and manage your plan.
  • To send account-related emails (verification, password reset, notifications) — we use Resend for email delivery.

3. AI processing

Conversation text you paste or sync is sent to a third-party AI provider (OpenAI or Groq, depending on configuration) to generate the analysis LOOP shows you — bottleneck detection, suggested next actions, and follow-up drafts. This text is sent solely to generate your results and is not used by us to train any AI model.

4. Third parties we share data with

We don't sell your data. We share the minimum necessary information with:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • OpenAI / Groq — AI analysis of conversation text you submit
  • Resend — transactional email delivery
  • Meta (WhatsApp/Messenger) and Google (Gmail), Slack — only if you explicitly connect these accounts, to read the messages needed for analysis

5. Data storage and security

  • Data is stored in a Postgres database with per-user access isolation — one user's data is never visible to another.
  • Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text).
  • OAuth tokens for connected accounts (Gmail, Slack, WhatsApp, Messenger) are encrypted at rest.
  • All traffic to LOOP is served over HTTPS.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can disconnect any integration (Gmail, Slack, WhatsApp, Messenger) at any time from the Integrations screen — this revokes our access immediately.
  • You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us (below).
  • You can cancel your subscription at any time; your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period.

7. Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is removed from our production database, except where we're required to retain billing records for legal/tax purposes.

8. Children's privacy

LOOP is a business tool and is not directed at, or intended for use by, anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as LOOP evolves. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of LOOP after changes means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at support@loop.bonto.run.
