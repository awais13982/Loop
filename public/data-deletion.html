Delete your data

You can delete your LOOP account and all of your data at any time.

Option 1: inside LOOP (instant)

  1. Log in to LOOP at loop.bonto.run.
  2. Open More and tap Delete my account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.

This permanently deletes your clients, loops, inbox messages, reminders and connected accounts (Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Messenger, WhatsApp), including stored access tokens, and cancels any active subscription.

Option 2: by email

Email awaisnazir030@gmail.com from the address you registered with and ask for your data to be deleted. We will delete it within 30 days.

Disconnecting a single service

In LOOP, go to More → Integrations and tap Disconnect next to the service. This removes the stored access token. You can also remove LOOP from your Google, Microsoft, Slack or Facebook account settings.

Facebook users: this page is LOOP's data deletion instructions for Meta.
